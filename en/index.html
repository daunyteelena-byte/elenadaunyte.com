About

Music as a journey, not a destination

Elena Daunytė is a young Lithuanian cellist and vocalist, active as both a soloist and chamber musician. She graduated from the M. K. Čiurlionis School of Art, continued her studies at the Royal Northern College of Music in Manchester, and is currently completing her master's degree at the Lithuanian Academy of Music and Theatre in the class of Prof. Rimantas Armonas.

She has refined her craft with teachers such as Giovanni Sollima, David Geringas, Frans Helmerson, and Troels Svane. Since 2016, Elena has also studied voice with Prof. V. Prudnikov and has repeatedly taken the stage as a vocalist.

Beyond the stage, Elena writes poetry on musical themes, improvises, composes, and runs marathons — for her, growth and the joy found in the process of improving matter more than abstract perfection.

„What matters most is growth — and the deepest joy found in the process of improving itself.“
Elena Daunytė

Awards and honours

Elena is a laureate of numerous international competitions, and for her active concert and competition career promoting Lithuania's name abroad, she has received personal commendations from the Presidents of the Republic of Lithuania.

  • Commendation from President Valdas Adamkus for promoting Lithuania's name worldwide
  • Commendation from President Dalia Grybauskaitė for active artistic work
  • Laureate of numerous international cello competitions
